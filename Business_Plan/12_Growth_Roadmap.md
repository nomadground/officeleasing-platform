# NMD Growth Roadmap v1.0

## Document Purpose

This document defines NMD's phased growth strategy, execution priorities, and long-term expansion roadmap.

This roadmap is not a fixed timeline.

It provides strategic direction for:

- AI Collaboration
- Product Development
- Business Prioritization
- Operation Scaling
- Investment Planning
- Decision Making

---

# 1. Growth Philosophy

## Build Demand First.
## Control Assets Later.

NMD does not scale by acquiring inventory first.

NMD scales by building:

- Corporate demand
- Operational knowledge
- Housing data
- Brand trust

Asset control increases only after demand validation.

---

# Growth Principle

Do not build everything.

Build what the current stage requires.
