# NMD Financial Model v1.0

## Document Purpose

This document defines NMD's financial structure, revenue model, key metrics, and growth economics.

This is not a fixed financial forecast.

It provides strategic financial context for:

- Business Planning
- KPI Management
- Product Development
- Investment Planning
- Data Systems
- Decision Making

# 4. Revenue Stream 1

# Partner Inventory Revenue

## Description

NMD connects demand with existing housing operators.

---

## Revenue Sources

Examples:

- Contract success fee
- Referral commission
- Platform fee
- Marketing fee

---

## Characteristics

Advantages:

- Low capital requirement
- Fast growth
- Inventory expansion

---

Limitations:

- Lower margin
- Less operational control

---

## Strategic Purpose

Acquire:

- Customers
- Corporate accounts
- Demand data

---

# 5. Revenue Stream 2

# Managed Inventory Revenue

## Description

NMD operates properties for owners.

---

## Revenue Sources

Examples:

- Management fee
- Operation fee
- Revenue share

---

## Characteristics

Advantages:

- Higher margin
- Better control
- Brand consistency

---

Creates:

Operational data

+

Customer experience control

---

# 6. Revenue Stream 3

# Master Lease Revenue

## Description

NMD leases properties and operates them directly.

---

## Revenue Formula

Customer Revenue

-

Lease Cost

-

Operating Expense

=

Operating Profit

---

## Advantages

- Highest brand control
- Higher profit potential
- Strong customer experience

---

## Risks

Requires:

- Deposit capital
- Occupancy management
- Pricing strategy

---

Expansion should be based on:

Validated Demand

not speculation.
