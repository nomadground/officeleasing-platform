- Marketing

# 1. Customer Philosophy

NMD is not a traditional rental marketplace.

Traditional real estate platforms focus on:

Tenant

↔

Property


NMD operates as a Corporate Housing ecosystem connecting:

Companies

+

Residents

+

Property Owners

+

Housing Operators


NMD serves multiple customer groups through one integrated platform.

# 3. Primary Customer

# Corporate Clients

## Definition

Organizations that need housing solutions for employees.

Corporate clients are NMD's most important customer segment.

---

## Examples

- Global corporations
- Korean enterprises
- Startups
- Technology companies
- Financial institutions
- Professional firms
- Medical companies
- Research organizations

---

## Decision Makers

Actual buyers:

- HR Managers
- General Affairs Teams
- Global Mobility Teams
- Office Managers
- Executives

---

## Problems

Companies struggle with:

- Finding reliable housing
- Managing multiple contracts
- Supporting foreign employees
- Short relocation timelines
- Housing quality inconsistency
- Administrative workload

---

## NMD Solution

NMD provides:

- Verified housing inventory
- Corporate-friendly contracts
- Furnished living
- Employee support
- Centralized housing management
