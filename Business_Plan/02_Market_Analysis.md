# 4. Korea Market Opportunity

## An Undeveloped Corporate Housing Market

Unlike the US and Europe, Korea has limited Corporate Housing infrastructure.

Reasons:

- Historical Jeonse system
- Individual landlord structure
- Company-owned housing culture
- Limited institutional rental operators

However, market conditions are changing.
