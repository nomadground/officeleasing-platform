# 7. Why Corporate Housing Has Not Developed In Korea

The opportunity exists because the market is structurally difficult.

---

## Barrier 1

Fragmented Ownership

Most housing:

- Individual owners
- Different conditions
- Different standards

---

## Barrier 2

Complex Housing System

Requires understanding:

- Lease contracts
- Corporate requirements
- Foreign residents
- Regulations
- Operations

---

## Barrier 3

Operational Complexity

Managing housing requires:

- Customer service
- Maintenance
- Cleaning
- Contract management
- Quality control

---

Complexity becomes NMD's opportunity.
