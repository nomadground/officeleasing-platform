# NMD Business Model v1.0

## Document Purpose

This document defines how NMD creates value, generates revenue, and scales its Corporate Housing & Furnished Living platform.

It provides strategic context for:

- Business Strategy
- Product Development
- AI Systems
- Operations
- Investment Planning
- Financial Modeling

---

# 1. Business Model Philosophy

## Demand First.
## Asset Control Later.

NMD does not start by owning or controlling every property.

The first objective is:

- Build corporate demand
- Build customer relationships
- Build housing data
- Build brand trust

Asset ownership and operational control increase over time.

---
